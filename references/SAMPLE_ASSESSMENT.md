# CAMP Sample Assessment

## English

### CAMP Result

**Organization:** Example Business Unit  
**Stage:** Stage 3 — Connected AI  
**Score:** 55 / 100  
**Confidence:** High

**Diagnosis:** individual AI use and enterprise connectivity are relatively strong, but reusable skills and session knowledge do not yet compound into organizational intelligence.

### Five dimensions
- AI Access: **15 / 20**
- AI Delegation: **15 / 20**
- Enterprise Connection: **15 / 20**
- Knowledge Compounding: **5 / 20**
- Role Transformation: **5 / 20**

**Strongest:** Access, Delegation, Connection — AI is already used for real work and connected to internal context/tools.  
**Weakest:** Compounding, Transformation — know-how is not yet reused systematically and handoffs remain largely unchanged.

### Biggest bottleneck
> Individual Intelligence → Organizational Intelligence

### Next 90 days
1. Convert the highest-value recurring workflows into governed skills with owner/version/eval.
2. Complete end-to-end context → judgment → tool → action for three priority workflows.
3. Capture reusable decisions and outcome knowledge from agent sessions and measure reuse.

### Recommended project
**Stage 4 pilot:** 10 power users, one repeated workflow, shared skill repository, version/eval, reuse measurement.

### Stop Doing
Do not use the number of vibe-coded dashboards or AI apps as the primary AX KPI. Separate `AI-made IT` from work that increases Agent Maturity.

**AI Operations:** Strong  
**Sovereign AI:** API  
**Next target:** Stage 4 — Compounding AI

If multiple assessments from Example Company exist, also show the company's mean/median and team/function dispersion. Other organizations remain anonymous.

### CAMP 결과

**조직:** Example Business Unit

### 5개 영역
- AI Access: **15 / 20**
- AI Delegation: **15 / 20**
- Enterprise Connection: **15 / 20**

**약점:** Compounding, Transformation — 노하우의 체계적 재사용과 Handoff 변화는 아직 제한적임.

### 가장 큰 병목
> Individual Intelligence → Organizational Intelligence

1. 가치가 높은 반복 Workflow를 Owner/Version/Eval이 있는 Skill로 전환.
2. 우선순위 업무 3개에서 Context → 판단 → Tool → Action을 End-to-End로 연결.
3. Agent Session의 핵심 판단과 Outcome Knowledge를 재사용 자산으로 남기고 재사용률 측정.

### 추천 과제
**Stage 4 Pilot:** Power User 10명, 반복 Workflow 1개, 공용 Skill 저장소, Version/Eval, 재사용 측정.

Vibe Coding Dashboard나 AI App 개수를 핵심 AX KPI로 관리하지 않습니다. `AI-made IT`와 Agent Maturity를 높이는 과제를 구분합니다.

**AI Operations:** Strong

**다음 목표:** Stage 4 — Compounding AI
